# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone participating in ai-phone issues, pull requests, reviews
and discussions, including maintainers.

- Criticism of code, design decisions and potential misuse is welcome. Explain
  the concern and provide evidence or a concrete example where possible.
- Keep disagreement about the work. Personal attacks, threats, harassment,
  discriminatory abuse and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.
- Do not publish someone else's private information without their permission.
- Respect requests to stop disruptive behaviour. Do not follow people into other
  threads or accounts to continue an argument.

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when these expectations are breached. The response will
depend on the behaviour and its impact; serious incidents do not require a prior
warning.

To report abuse without posting it publicly, follow
[GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Use **Report to repository admins** when that option is available, or **Report
abuse to GitHub Support**. Include links and enough context to identify the
behaviour. You do not have to confront the person or open a public issue.

For vulnerabilities in the software, use [the security policy](SECURITY.md).
